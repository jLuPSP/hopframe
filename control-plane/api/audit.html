Audit reports

Every report you download from this page is bound to the current state of the append-only audit log via a cryptographic chain proof in its trailer. That proof binds the export to a specific point in chain history; an auditor (or you, six months from now) can re-walk the chain over the included records and confirm none were altered after the fact. No other tool in this category includes export-time integrity proof by default.
