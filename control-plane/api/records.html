Audit records

Every record is hash-chained to its predecessor; the chain head binds the whole log. With a signing key configured, each record carries a per-record signature over its canonical bytes, so a single record can be disclosed to an auditor without revealing adjacent records. The Merkle proof ties the record to a snapshot window and lets the auditor verify membership without the rest of the chain. Verify a record below; the verification runs in your browser using the public key the control plane returned.
