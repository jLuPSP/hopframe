Sensor fleet

Every sensor heartbeats with its binary version, applied policy version, and applied content version. Rows that have not heartbeat in the last 60 seconds are flagged stale. Sensors whose applied versions trail the active versions on the control plane are flagged as drift; that almost always means the sensor cannot reach the control plane or is wedged. The fleet view is the operator's first look when a policy change does not appear to take effect.
