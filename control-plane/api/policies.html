Policies

A rule matches; a policy disposes. Policies bind a scope (org default, tenant, sensor, or specific MCP server) plus a selector (which findings to act on) to a disposition (monitor, warn, or block). Most-specific scope wins; among ties, the strongest mode wins. Sensors hot-reload changes on their next heartbeat.
